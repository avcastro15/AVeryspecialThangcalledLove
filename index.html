    Quick guidance

    Polished, yet comfortable

    Our wedding motif is wine red, dusty blue, ivory, and champagne. To keep the celebration cohesive, we kindly ask guests to dress in the muted green tones above.

    • Lovely choices: midi dresses, jumpsuits, soft tailoring, coordinated separates, polos, button-downs, loafers, and clean minimalist sneakers.
    • Please avoid: jeans, t-shirts, slippers, athletic shoes, bright colors, and colors outside the palette.
    A gentle request

    White is reserved for the bride

    Please do not wear white, ivory, cream, off-white, or anything that may photograph as white.
